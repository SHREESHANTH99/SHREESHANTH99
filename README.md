### 🌌 About

Hi, I'm Shreeshanth — a full-stack developer and B.Tech student at **IIITDM Jabalpur**. I got into systems engineering by chasing "why": APIs, data flow, real-time behavior, and the edge cases that only show up under load.

Outside engineering, I follow emerging technology and anime, and I'm working deliberately toward a long-term goal of living and working in Japan.

> I don't just want to use systems. I want to understand them, and eventually, design better ones.
