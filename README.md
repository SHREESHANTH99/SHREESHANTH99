## About
Hi! I'm Shreeshanth, a Full stack developer and B.Tech student at IIITDM Jabalpur.
I got into systems engineering not by accident — I'm just genuinely curious about how things work under the hood. APIs, data flow, real-time behaviour, the edge cases that break everything at 2am. That stuff fascinates me.

Currently a B.Tech student at **IIITDM Jabalpur**. Outside engineering I like to watch anime and chase new technology — which, honestly, is how I ended up here.

One long-term goal: live and work in Japan. I'm building toward that deliberately.

> *I don't just want to use systems. I want to understand them, and eventually, design better ones.*
